# Forsaken Shores Mod 🏝️

> A new horizon for Unturned.

**Forsaken Shores** is a project based on the source code of **Unturned 3.0**, created with the goal of exploring a new survival experience within the game's universe.

The project was born from the desire to preserve and expand a game that was part of its developers' childhood, taking advantage of the source code becoming available to create new experiences and contribute to keeping the community active.

## 🌊 Concept

Forsaken Shores aims to combine Unturned's traditional survival experience with elements of **exploration, navigation, and discovery**.

The goal is to use the game's existing mechanics as a foundation while introducing a new way to play, inspired by elements from games such as:

* The Forest
* Stranded Deep
* DayZ
* Sea of Thieves
* Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag

The goal is not to turn Unturned into a pirate game or move it to another era. Instead, the project aims to introduce navigation and exploration mechanics into the existing universe of the game.

## 🏝️ Survival and Exploration

The experience is based on the idea of a survivor arriving in an unknown region after an accident.

From that moment on, the player must use Unturned's existing survival mechanics while exploring the island and its surroundings.

Maritime exploration will play an important role, allowing players to:

* Explore different islands;
* Navigate the ocean;
* Discover unknown locations;
* Explore abandoned vessels and areas;
* Discover elements related to the world's story.

The goal is to make the ocean more than just an area to travel through and turn it into part of the survival experience itself.

## 🧟 Zombies

Zombies remain a fundamental part of the experience.

In addition to Unturned's traditional zombies, the project aims to explore new possibilities related to the different environments encountered during exploration, including coastal areas, vessels, and other locations.

## 📖 Lore

Forsaken Shores also aims to expand the game's universe and story.

The narrative will be built primarily through exploration, allowing players to find clues and elements that help them understand what happened in that world.

The intention is not necessarily to explain everything, but to create a sense of discovery and mystery as the player explores new regions.

## 🗺️ A World to Explore

One of the project's main ideas is the creation of a world composed of different islands and regions.

Each location may feature different environments, structures, and situations, encouraging players to leave their starting area and explore the unknown.

The first experience of the project aims to represent exactly that:

> A small island, a survivor, limited resources, and an ocean full of possibilities.

## 🚧 Project Status

Forsaken Shores is currently in the early planning and development stage.

The project is still exploring its possibilities and aims to evolve gradually from the source code made available to the community.

The priority is to build the experience gradually, starting with small playable sections and expanding the project as new ideas and possibilities are discovered.
